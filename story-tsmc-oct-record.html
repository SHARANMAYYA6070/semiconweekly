TSMC's October sales jump 60% to a monthly high

The world's biggest contract chipmaker had its best month ever in October 2026, as demand for AI chips stays strong.

Semicon Weekly · 11 November 2026
ShareWhatsAppTelegram
In short
  • TSMC's sales in October 2026 were about NT$586.9 billion, its highest for any single month.
  • That is 59.7% more than October 2025 and 14.7% more than September 2026.
  • Sales from January to October 2026 were about NT$4,485.6 billion, up 43.3% from a year earlier.

What happened

  • TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company) shared its monthly sales report on 10 November 2026.
  • Its net revenue for October 2026 was about NT$586.89 billion. In September 2026 it was about NT$511.86 billion, so sales grew 14.7% in one month.
  • In October 2025, the figure was about NT$367.47 billion, so this October was 59.7% higher.
  • Total sales for the first ten months of 2026 reached about NT$4,485.62 billion, 43.3% more than the same months of 2025. TSMC makes chips for companies like Nvidia, Apple and AMD, and AI chips are a big part of its growth.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Monthly sales from TSMC are one of the clearest signs of how strong chip demand really is, because almost every big chip company uses its factories.
  • A record month shows that, so far, demand for AI chips is still growing, even as some investors worry spending may slow.

Why it is good for freshers

  • AI chip demand is still strong, so the design, verification and physical design teams in Bengaluru and Hyderabad that work on these chips stay busy.
  • Learning how chips go from design to a foundry like TSMC (tape-out, PDKs, process nodes) is useful in almost any chip job.
  • Following monthly numbers like these helps you understand the industry better in interviews.

Why it is good for India

  • Many chips made by TSMC are designed partly by engineering teams in India, so strong foundry sales support design work here.
  • India's own fabs, like Tata's plant in Dholera, are being built to join this global chip supply chain.

Words in this story

  • Foundry: A company that makes chips designed by other companies. TSMC is the biggest foundry in the world.
  • Net revenue: The money a company earns from sales in a period, after things like returns and discounts.
  • NT$: New Taiwan dollar, the currency of Taiwan.
