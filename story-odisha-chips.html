Odisha has five chip projects worth over $4 billion

Chip-making in India is spreading east. Odisha already hosts two "firsts" from India's chip mission.

Semicon Weekly · 31 October 2026
In short
  • Odisha says five chip projects worth more than $4 billion are coming to the state.
  • Two of India's 12 approved chip mission projects are already in Odisha: the first compound semiconductor fab and the first advanced 3D glass chip packaging unit.
  • The state says work on them started within 6 to 8 months of approval.

What happened

  • In August 2025, the Union Cabinet approved two chip projects in Info Valley, Bhubaneswar, under the India Semiconductor Mission.
  • SiCSem, with UK partner Clas-SiC, is building India's first commercial compound semiconductor fab. It will make silicon carbide (SiC) chips for EVs, solar inverters, defence and data centres.
  • 3D Glass Solutions (3DGS) is building an advanced packaging unit that uses glass layers to join many chips into one powerful module, for AI, defence and cars.
  • At the end of October 2026, Odisha said a total of five chip projects worth over $4 billion are now coming to the state.

Planned yearly capacity of the two approved plants

  • SiCSem: 60,000 wafers and 96 million packaged chips.
  • 3DGS: 69,600 glass panel substrates, 50 million assembled units and 13,200 3D chip modules.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • SiC chips handle high power and heat, which EVs, chargers and solar power need.
  • Advanced packaging is how modern AI chips are built, by stacking and joining smaller chips.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Fabs and packaging plants hire process, equipment, test, quality and reliability engineers, not only designers.
  • Learn: semiconductor device basics, power electronics, and packaging terms (substrate, interposer, chiplet).
  • Students in eastern India may find chip jobs closer to home.

Why it is good for India

  • Chip-making is no longer only in Gujarat and Karnataka; more states are joining.
  • SiC and glass packaging are new technologies where India can start early.

Words in this story

  • Compound semiconductor: a chip material made of two or more elements, like silicon carbide (SiC), instead of only silicon.
  • Packaging: putting a bare chip into a protective case and connecting it to the outside world.
  • 3D heterogeneous integration: stacking and joining different chips into one module.
