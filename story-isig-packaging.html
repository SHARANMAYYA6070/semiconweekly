Chip group ISIG says India is next big place for packaging

At its first India summit, the global chip group said AI has made packaging important again.

Semicon Weekly · 3 November 2026
In short
  • The International Semiconductor Industry Group (ISIG) is holding its first India executive summit in New Delhi.
  • ISIG's CEO Salah Nasri said chip packaging is important again because of AI, and India is the next country to grow in it.
  • ISIG India chapter head Santosh Kumar said India has a big chance in packaging, testing (OSAT) and power chips.

What happened

  • ISIG, a global chip industry group, held its first India executive summit in New Delhi.
  • CEO Salah Nasri said AI chips have made packaging important again, and India is the next country to grow in it.
  • Santosh Kumar, head of ISIG's India chapter, said India has a big chance in packaging, testing and power chips.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • AI chips are built by joining many smaller chips inside one advanced package.
  • Packaging and testing plants can be built faster than full chip factories.

Why it is good for freshers

  • India's new packaging and testing plants will need many engineers, so packaging, testing and power device skills are smart areas to learn.
  • Learn: chiplets, interposers, wire bonding, flip-chip and test basics.
  • Mechanical, materials and ECE graduates all fit packaging roles.

Why it is good for India

  • Packaging and testing is where India's chip plants are starting first.
  • Power chips (SiC, GaN) are needed for EVs and solar, both growing in India.

Words in this story

  • OSAT: Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test, companies that package and test chips.
  • Chiplet: a small chip that is joined with others to build one big chip.
