Tips for freshers

  • Fresher chip jobs are fewer than senior ones. Apply early, and also look at internships, trainee and support roles to get in.
  • Put one strong project on your resume: an RTL design with a testbench, or an OpenLane flow to GDSII.
  • Read the experience line before applying. "0–1 yr" or "freshers eligible" means you can apply.
  • Know what to expect on pay: read our VLSI fresher salary guide, follow the DV or RTL design roadmap, or browse jobs by company.
