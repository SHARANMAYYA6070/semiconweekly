IIT Guwahati and Tata Electronics to start chip packaging courses

A new Centre of Excellence will train students for Tata's chip assembly and test plant in Assam.

Semicon Weekly · 1 November 2026
In short
  • IIT Guwahati and Tata Electronics signed an agreement to build a Centre of Excellence for chip packaging and testing.
  • IIT Guwahati will run diploma and B.Tech-level programmes with hands-on training.
  • Students will get practical training at Tata Electronics, to build skilled people for Tata's plant in Jagiroad, Assam.

Key numbers

₹27,000 crTata's chip assembly and test plant, Jagiroad
2Course levels: diploma and B.Tech
1New Centre of Excellence

What happened

  • IIT Guwahati and Tata Electronics signed an agreement (MoU).
  • They will set up a Centre of Excellence (CoE) focused on chip packaging and testing.
  • IIT Guwahati will offer diploma and B.Tech-level programmes with practical, hands-on training.
  • Students will also train at Tata Electronics, which is building a ₹27,000 crore chip assembly and test plant in Jagiroad, Assam.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • A plant needs trained people on day one. This builds them in advance.
  • Courses designed with the company teach exactly the skills the plant needs.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Students from Assam and the Northeast can now train for chip packaging jobs close to home, with a direct link to Tata's new plant.
  • Packaging and testing roles suit ECE, EEE, mechanical and materials graduates.
  • Learn: wire bonding, flip-chip, test basics, and quality and reliability terms.

Why it is good for India

  • It builds a chip workforce in the Northeast, not only in big cities.
  • Industry and IIT working together is how strong chip regions grow.

Words in this story

  • Centre of Excellence (CoE): a special lab and training centre focused on one field.
  • OSAT / ATMP: plants that assemble, test and package chips.
  • MoU: an agreement to work together.
