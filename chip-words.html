Chip words A–Z

Every chip and semiconductor word from our news stories, explained in simple English. New words are added with every story.

2nm
A name for a very advanced chip-making generation. Smaller means more transistors in the same space.Seen in: HCLTech opens two chip centres in Bengaluru and Hubballi for Rapidus
