Madhya Pradesh chip summit brings ₹15,425 crore proposals, 14,000+ jobs

A two-day summit in Indore ended with big investment plans and a Centre of Excellence for chips.

Semicon Weekly · 2 November 2026
In short
  • A two-day semiconductor summit in Indore ended with investment proposals worth over ₹15,425 crore.
  • More than 14,450 jobs are expected, and five companies got land letters.
  • The state will also set up a Semiconductor Centre of Excellence.

Key numbers

₹15,425 cr+Investment proposals
14,450+Expected jobs
5Companies that got land letters
2 daysSummit in Indore

What happened

  • Madhya Pradesh held a two-day semiconductor summit in Indore.
  • It ended with investment proposals worth more than ₹15,425 crore and over 14,450 expected jobs.
  • Five companies received land allotment letters.
  • Big plans include an electronics plant by Una Ecosystems and chip work by Spintronics AI and Lalwala Industries. The state will also set up a Semiconductor Centre of Excellence.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Land letters mean companies can start building, not just talk.
  • A Centre of Excellence helps train local talent for these companies.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Chip and electronics jobs are coming to central India too, so students from MP may soon find good roles in Indore and Ujjain.
  • Proposals take time to become jobs, so build skills now: electronics manufacturing, testing and embedded systems.
  • Watch for internships linked to the new Centre of Excellence.

Why it is good for India

  • Chip and electronics work spreads to more regions, not only a few big cities.
  • More local plants mean India imports fewer electronics parts.

Words in this story

  • Investment proposal: a company's plan to invest; it becomes real only when they build.
  • Land letter: a letter from the state allotting land to a company for its plant.
