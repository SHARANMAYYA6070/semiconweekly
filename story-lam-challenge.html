3,000 students compete in Lam Research's chip challenge

A national contest by chip-equipment maker Lam Research gave students a taste of chip manufacturing problems.

Semicon Weekly · 7 November 2026
ShareWhatsAppTelegram
In short
  • More than 3,000 engineering students from about 190 colleges took part in the Lam Research Challenge 2026.
  • Out of 803 teams, 25 teams from 20 institutions reached the final.
  • The two-day final was held at PES University, Bengaluru, on 5–6 November.

Key numbers

3,000+Students who took part
803Teams that entered
25Teams in the final

What happened

  • Lam Research, a company that makes machines used to make chips, ran the Lam Research Challenge 2026 for engineering students.
  • More than 3,000 students from about 190 colleges took part in 803 teams.
  • 25 teams from 20 institutions reached the two-day final at PES University, Bengaluru, on 5–6 November.
  • The contest aims to prepare students for chip manufacturing work.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Chip factories need engineers who can solve real manufacturing problems, not only design chips.
  • Contests like this connect colleges with chip-equipment companies.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Contests like this let students show real problem-solving skills to a big chip equipment company, which helps when applying for jobs.
  • Watch for next year's edition and similar contests from chip companies; they are open to engineering students.
  • Add contest results to your resume under Achievements, with what your team built.

Why it is good for India

  • More students trained in chip manufacturing helps India's new fabs and packaging plants find people.
  • Global equipment makers working with Indian colleges builds local skills.

  • Chip equipment: the machines used inside a chip factory to make and check chips.
  • Finalist: a team chosen to compete in the last round of a contest.
  • Chip manufacturing: the steps of making chips in a factory (fab), from wafers to finished chips.
