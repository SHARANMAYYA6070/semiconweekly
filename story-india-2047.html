India sets goal to be a chip powerhouse by 2047

The defence ministry says real self-reliance in chips means design, testing and making, all in India.

Semicon Weekly · 31 October 2026
In short
  • The defence ministry says India wants to be a strong, self-reliant chip country by 2047.
  • It shared this after ESTIC 2026, a big science and technology meeting in New Delhi, where DRDO led a session on chips.
  • The message: real self-reliance needs chip design, testing and large-scale production, not just factories.

Key numbers

2047Target year (100 years of independence)
27–29 OctESTIC 2026, New Delhi
12Government science ministries and departments taking part
3Steps India must master: design, test, make

What happened

  • ESTIC 2026 (Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave) was held in New Delhi from 27 to 29 October.
  • DRDO, India's defence research body, led a session called "Semiconductors and Next-Gen Chips".
  • Micron India's head, Anand Ramamoorthy, gave the main talk on AI chips, smart "edge" chips and microprocessors. Experts from Qualcomm, Tata and others joined a panel.
  • Afterwards, the defence ministry said India aims to be a globally competitive chip powerhouse by 2047, and that this needs strength in design, testing and production.

The three steps, in simple words

  • Design: planning the chip on computers (RTL, verification, physical design).
  • Test: checking every chip works before it goes into a product.
  • Make: printing chips on wafers in a fab and packaging them, at large scale.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Defence systems like radar, missiles and secure communication need chips India can trust.
  • A clear long-term goal helps companies and colleges plan.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Defence and government chip work needs Indian engineers who can design and test chips, so these skills will stay in demand for years.
  • Watch for openings at DRDO labs, BEL, ISRO and their partner companies.
  • Skills to build: Verilog/SystemVerilog, verification (UVM), and chip testing (DFT) basics.

Why it is good for India

  • Making our own chips protects India from supply problems and outside pressure.
  • India keeps more of the value when it designs and makes chips, not only uses them.

Words in this story

  • DRDO: Defence Research and Development Organisation, India's defence research agency.
  • Self-reliant (Atmanirbhar): able to do something without depending on other countries.
  • Edge chip: a chip that does smart work inside a device, like a camera, instead of in a data centre.
