Marvell to buy Lumentum in $115 billion AI deal

AI data centres need fast light-based links, and Marvell wants to make more of them itself.

Semicon Weekly · 3 November 2026
In short
  • US chip company Marvell will buy Lumentum, which makes lasers and optical parts for data centres.
  • The all-stock deal is worth about $115 billion.
  • The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2028.

Key numbers

$115 bnDeal value (all stock)
H1 2028Expected closing
2ndIndia's rank among Marvell's R&D bases

What happened

  • Marvell agreed to buy Lumentum in an all-stock deal worth about $115 billion.
  • Lumentum makes lasers and optical parts used in data centres.
  • AI data centres need fast, light-based links to move huge amounts of data between chips.
  • The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2028, after approvals.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Moving data with light is faster and uses less power than copper wires.
  • Owning both chips and optics lets Marvell build complete AI networking products.

Why it is good for freshers

  • India is Marvell's second-largest R&D base and it plans to double its India team, so high-speed and optical chip skills could mean more jobs in Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
  • Learn about high-speed links: SerDes, signal integrity, and networking basics.
  • Analog and mixed-signal skills are in demand for these chips.

Why it is good for India

  • Big global deals often bring more engineering work to India teams.
  • India gains skills in advanced AI networking chips.

Words in this story

  • All-stock deal: paying with the buyer's shares instead of cash.
  • Optical link: sending data as light through fibre instead of electricity through wires.
  • SerDes: a circuit that sends data very fast over a few wires.
