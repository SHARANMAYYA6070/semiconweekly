IIT Madras alumni's start-up offers $1 million for chip materials puzzles

The start-up uses AI to find new materials for chips. It picked five open problems that even frontier AI agents could not crack after days of work, and put prize money on each.

Semicon Weekly · 10 November 2026
ShareWhatsAppTelegram
In short
  • Discovered Materials, started by IIT Madras graduates Advaith Sridhar and Akash Ramdas, is offering up to $1 million in prizes.
  • The money is split across five hard materials problems: $400,000, $200,000, $200,000, $100,000 and $100,000.
  • One problem is about what happens electrically where two semiconductor layers meet.

Key numbers

$1 milliontotal prize pool
$400,000biggest single prize
5open problems in materials science

What happened

  • Discovered Materials announced the challenge on 5 November 2026. The company uses AI to search for new materials for chips.
  • It was co-founded by IIT Madras graduates Advaith Sridhar and Akash Ramdas, and is based in San Francisco.
  • It says frontier AI agents could not solve these five problems even after days of working on their own.
  • Prizes are $400,000, $200,000, $200,000, $100,000 and $100,000. One problem deals with the electrical behaviour at the boundary between two semiconductor layers.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Chips keep getting better partly through new materials, not only smaller transistors.
  • Interfaces between layers decide how well transistors, solar cells and LEDs work, so solving them has real value.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Better chips now depend on materials science too, so physics and materials skills can open research careers in the chip world.
  • Semiconductor device physics (band diagrams, junctions, interfaces) is worth learning well — it shows up in fab and process interviews.
  • AI + science is a new career mix: Python plus device or materials knowledge is a rare combination.

Why it is good for India

  • Founders from Indian colleges are building deep-tech chip start-ups, which inspires students here.
  • India's new fabs and packaging plants will also need materials and process engineers.

Words in this story

  • Materials science: The study of how materials are built and behave, and how to make new ones.
  • Interface: The boundary where two layers meet; in chips, its electrical behaviour can make or break a device.
  • AI agent: An AI program that can plan and run many steps on its own, like running simulations and checking results.
