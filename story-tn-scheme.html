Tamil Nadu launches its own semiconductor scheme

Chip projects approved by the Centre will get extra help from the state, and a chip park is planned near Chennai.

Semicon Weekly · 2 November 2026
In short
  • Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay launched the Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Ecosystem Scheme 2026.
  • Chip projects approved by the Centre will get extra support from the state, and other useful projects can get custom help.
  • A semiconductor and electronics park is planned at Maduramangalam in Kancheepuram district.

Key numbers

2026Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Ecosystem Scheme
ExtraState support on top of central support
1Chip and electronics park planned (Maduramangalam)

What happened

  • Chief Minister Vijay launched the Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Ecosystem Scheme 2026.
  • Projects approved under the central chip mission will get additional support from the state.
  • Other projects that help the chip ecosystem can get custom (case-by-case) support.
  • A semiconductor and electronics park is planned at Maduramangalam in Kancheepuram district, near Chennai.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • State support on top of central support makes a state more attractive to chip companies.
  • A ready park helps companies set up faster.

Why it is good for freshers

  • More chip factories and parks in Tamil Nadu could mean fab, packaging and testing jobs near Chennai for students in the south.
  • Chennai already has a big electronics manufacturing base, so many roles may come in manufacturing and quality.
  • Skills to build: semiconductor process basics, testing, and quality tools.

Why it is good for India

  • More states joining the chip race brings more investment to India.
  • Tamil Nadu's electronics strength can help India make complete products.

Words in this story

  • Ecosystem: all the companies, suppliers, colleges and services that work together in one industry.
  • Electronics park: an area with ready land and utilities for electronics companies.
