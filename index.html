In Short

Govt clears ₹1.25 lakh crore for Chip Mission 2.0

short by Semicon Weekly / 15 Jul 2026

India's chip mission is getting a much bigger budget for its second phase: ₹1.25 lakh crore, up from ₹76,000 crore. The programme period is now 12 years instead of 5. The money also goes to machines, materials, chemicals and Indian chip designs. Design companies will get special focus, which could bring more design and verification jobs.

Tata's chip plant to have 450 suppliers in a 363-acre park

short by Semicon Weekly / 18 Sep 2026

Tata Electronics expects about 450 Indian and foreign suppliers to support its chip factory in Dholera, Gujarat. It is building a 363-acre ready-to-use vendor park for them. A fab cannot stop even for a few hours, so repair teams, spare parts, chemicals and gases must be close by. This can create many engineering jobs beyond chip design.

What is tape-out? The big day in a chip's life

short by Semicon Weekly / 28 Oct 2026

Tape-out is the moment a finished, fully checked chip design is handed over to make photomasks, the stencils used to print the chip. After that, a wafer spends about three months in the factory. A mistake found later means fixing and taping out again, which costs months and money. That is why verification engineers are so important.
