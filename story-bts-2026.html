Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026 on 17–19 Nov has a chip track

Karnataka's big tech event returns to BIEC with a special track for electronics and chips.

Semicon Weekly · 3 November 2026
In short
  • The 29th Bengaluru Tech Summit will be held at BIEC in Bengaluru from 17 to 19 November.
  • It is run by the Karnataka government, and this year's theme is "AI & Beyond".
  • There is a special Electro-Semicon track and a Circuit Stage with talks on electronics and chips.

Key numbers

17–19 NovSummit dates
29thEdition of the summit
BIECBangalore International Exhibition Centre

What happened

  • The Karnataka government is holding the 29th Bengaluru Tech Summit from 17 to 19 November 2026.
  • It will be at BIEC (Bangalore International Exhibition Centre).
  • This year's theme is "AI & Beyond".
  • A special Electro-Semicon track and a Circuit Stage will host talks on electronics and chips. Embedded World India also runs at the same summit.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Many chip and electronics companies show their work in one place.
  • Talks explain where the industry is going and what skills it needs.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Students can meet chip companies and start-ups in one place, learn what skills they want, and build contacts in India's biggest chip design city.
  • Carry a one-page resume and a short project list to share.
  • Check the official site for student passes and registration.

Why it is good for India

  • It shows Bengaluru as India's chip design hub to the world.
  • It connects start-ups, big companies, colleges and the government.

Words in this story

  • BIEC: Bangalore International Exhibition Centre, a big venue on Tumakuru Road.
  • Track: a set of talks on one topic at a big event.
