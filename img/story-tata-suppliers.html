Tata's chip plant will need 450 suppliers, and is building a 363-acre park for them

India's first big chip factory, in Dholera, Gujarat, needs many partner companies close by to keep running.

Semicon Weekly · Reported 18 September 2026
In short
  • About 450 Indian and foreign suppliers are expected to support Tata Electronics' chip factory in Dholera.
  • Tata is building a 363-acre "plug-and-play" vendor park next to it, so suppliers can set up fast.
  • A chip factory cannot stop even for a few hours, so repair teams, spare parts, chemicals and gases must be nearby.

Key numbers

450Suppliers expected
363 acresSize of the vendor park
₹91,000 crAbout the cost of the Dholera fab
7Partnership deals Tata signed at SEMICON India 2026

What happened

  • At SEMICON India 2026, Utpal Shah, a senior vice president at Tata Electronics, said about 450 suppliers will support the Dholera fab.
  • Tata is developing a 363-acre vendor park with a Singapore-based developer. It will have ready power, water, roads and, in some cases, buildings.
  • Foreign suppliers can buy land in the park.
  • Tata also announced partnerships, including Fujifilm for a materials plant (about ₹700 crore, in phases) and Nexperia to make its power chips at the new fab.
  • Tata's CEO said the Dholera fab and the Assam packaging plant are on track.

What suppliers will do

  • Repair and maintenance of very costly chip-making machines.
  • Spare parts kept close by so machines are fixed fast.
  • Chemicals and gases of very high purity, used every day in the fab.
  • Logistics and warehousing to move materials safely.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • A fab is only as good as its supply chain. Suppliers next door mean less downtime and lower cost.
  • A ready-made park makes it easier and faster for global companies to come to India.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Hundreds of supplier companies will need engineers: equipment service, process support, quality, testing and safety.
  • ECE, EEE, mechanical and chemical engineering graduates can all find roles here, not only chip designers.
  • Tata also announced a talent-training partnership with Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya, L&T EduTech and Jacobs.

Why it is good for India

  • It builds a full chip "town" in Gujarat, not just one factory.
  • Indian vendors get a chance to learn from global suppliers and later serve other fabs.

Words in this story

  • Fab: a factory where chips are made on silicon wafers.
  • Vendor park: an area with ready land and utilities where suppliers set up near a big plant.
  • Plug-and-play: ready to use; a company can move in and start quickly.
