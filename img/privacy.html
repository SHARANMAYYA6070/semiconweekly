Privacy

We collect as little as possible. Here is exactly what happens when you visit or sign up.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Email sign-up

  • When you click Subscribe, you are sent to our Substack page to confirm. Your email is stored by Substack, not on this website.
  • We use it only to send the weekly Semicon Weekly email. We never sell or share it.
  • You can unsubscribe any time with the link at the bottom of every email. Substack's own privacy policy also applies.

Visitor counting

  • We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly tool. It uses no cookies and does not collect your name, email or exact location.
  • We only see totals, such as how many people opened a page and which country or device they came from.

Other services this site loads

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the jobs, news and events lists load from Google Sheets. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when they send the content.
  • Job, event and news links take you to other websites, which have their own privacy rules.

Questions

Write to us from the Contact page if you have any question about your data, or want us to remove anything.
