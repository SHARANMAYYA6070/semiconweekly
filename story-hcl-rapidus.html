HCLTech opens two chip centres in Bengaluru and Hubballi for Rapidus

Engineers in Karnataka will help Japan's Rapidus prepare to make some of the world's most advanced chips.

Semicon Weekly · 31 October 2026
In short
  • On 26 October 2026, HCLTech and Japan's Rapidus opened two new HCLTech development centres, one in Bengaluru and one in Hubballi.
  • Engineers there will do chip engineering work for Rapidus, such as mask design, layout design and process design kit (PDK) work.
  • HCLTech will also help build a group of trained Indian engineers for Rapidus's needs in Japan.

Key numbers

2New centres: Bengaluru and Hubballi
2nmThe chip size Rapidus plans to make
FY2027Rapidus target for mass production (second half)
5Work areas at the centres

What happened

  • Rapidus is a Japanese company building a factory to make very advanced logic chips (the "brain" chips in computers and AI systems).
  • On 26 October 2026, HCLTech and Rapidus opened two HCLTech centres in Karnataka just for Rapidus's engineering work.
  • Rapidus's CEO, Dr Atsuyoshi Koike, said a strong engineering team is key to its plans, and India gives it access to a large pool of engineers.
  • HCLTech's CEO, C Vijayakumar, said advanced chips will shape the next ten years of technology, and called this a sign of growing India–Japan teamwork.
  • The money involved and the number of engineers have not been shared.

What the centres will do

  • Mask design: preparing the stencils used to print each chip layer.
  • Layout design: drawing the real shapes of transistors and wires.
  • PDK development: building the "rule book and parts kit" that chip designers use for a factory.
  • DTCO: tuning the chip design and the factory process together for the best result.
  • Design infrastructure: the tools and computer setup that design teams work on.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Rapidus gets skilled engineers in India to support its work in Japan.
  • India gets hands-on work on one of the newest chip technologies in the world.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Layout, PDK and mask work need ECE/VLSI graduates. Learn layout basics (Virtuoso or Magic), DRC/LVS and device physics.
  • Hubballi is a tier-2 city, so chip jobs are growing beyond Bengaluru.
  • The talent pipeline for Japan may open chances to work abroad later.

Why it is good for India

  • It brings very advanced (2nm-class) chip know-how to Indian engineers.
  • It makes the India–Japan chip partnership stronger.

Words in this story

  • 2nm: a name for a very advanced chip-making generation. Smaller means more transistors in the same space.
  • PDK (process design kit): files and rules from a factory that tell designers how to draw chips it can make.
  • Layout: the exact drawing of a chip's shapes, layer by layer.
