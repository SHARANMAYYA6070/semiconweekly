Corrections policy

We try hard to be accurate. When we make a mistake, we fix it openly.

Semicon Weekly · Last updated 4 November 2026
Editorial policyCorrections policyTerms of usePrivacyContact

How to report a mistake

  • Email semiconweekly.team@gmail.com or use the contact form and choose Correction.
  • Tell us the page link, what is wrong, and if possible a source that shows the right information.
  • For a closed or broken job link, just tell us the job title. We will check it and remove it if it has closed.

What we do

  • We check every report against the original source.
  • If a fact is wrong, we correct the page and add a note at the end: "Corrected on [date]: [what changed]."
  • Small fixes to spelling or wording that do not change the meaning are made without a note.
  • If a whole story turns out to be wrong, we remove it from the homepage and explain why on its page.
  • We aim to reply within two working days.

Jobs and events

Job openings and event dates change quickly. Always confirm on the official page before you apply or travel. If you find a job that has closed, or an event date that has changed, please tell us.
