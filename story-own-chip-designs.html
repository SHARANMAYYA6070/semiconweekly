Chip design

India must own chip designs, not just host fabs: AP official

Karthikeya Misra says India should move from giving land and power to foreign chip firms to owning chip designs and technology itself.

Semicon Weekly · 13 November 2026
ShareWhatsAppTelegram
In short
  • Karthikeya Misra, Secretary to the Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister, wants India to build at least one world-class, Indian-owned chip company in the next five years.
  • He says most hardware in India's new AI data centres is designed in the US and made in Taiwan.
  • This is his opinion and suggestion; no new government plan or funding was announced.

Key numbers

5 yearsTime Misra suggests for building one Indian-owned chip company
12Chip projects approved under India Semiconductor Mission, as per the article
2028Year India's first chip fab at Dholera is expected to start

What happened

  • Karthikeya Misra, Secretary to the Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister, shared his views in an article reported by The Hans India on 13 November.
  • He said India's growth in data centres and chips is a chance to build at least one globally competitive chip company owned by Indians within five years.
  • He said India should not only offer land, power, water and customers to foreign firms, but should own chip designs, IP and technology too.
  • He gave Nvidia as an example: it earns huge value from its designs, software and customers without owning chip factories.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • It shows that state officials are now talking about chip design ownership, not just building factories.
  • Owning designs and IP keeps more of the money and know-how inside India.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Indian-owned chip companies would need many design, verification and IP engineers in India, not only factory staff.
  • Strong RTL design, verification and physical design skills fit exactly the kind of work an Indian chip product company needs.
  • Freshers who learn how a full chip product is built, from spec to tape-out, will be ready if such companies grow.

Why it is good for India

  • An Indian-owned chip company would reduce dependence on chips designed in the US and made in Taiwan.
  • It would add high-value product jobs on top of the services and factory jobs India already has.

Words in this story

  • IP (intellectual property): Designs and ideas a company owns, such as a chip's blueprint, that others must pay to use
  • Fab: A factory that makes chips on silicon wafers
  • Fabless company: A chip company that designs chips but lets other factories make them, like Nvidia
