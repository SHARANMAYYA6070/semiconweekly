17–19Nov 2026

Embedded World India 2026, at Bengaluru Tech Summit

Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC), Bengaluru
Expo + conferenceEmbeddedNear Bengaluru
What it is
The first India edition of Embedded World, a big global show for embedded systems. It runs inside Bengaluru Tech Summit (BTS), Karnataka's yearly tech event.
What happens
An exhibition pavilion and a conference track on chips and modules, operating systems, AI and IoT. BTS also has tracks on AI and DeepTech, space tech, data centres and electric mobility, plus a startup programme.
How big
BTS expects 1,800+ exhibitors and 1,000+ startups from 75+ countries.
Who should go
ECE and EEE students, embedded and IoT freshers, anyone who wants to meet hardware companies.
Why go
See real products, talk to engineers at stalls, collect company contacts, and learn which skills companies want.
How to register
Visit the official Embedded World India page and the BTS website for visitor passes.

2–6Jan 2027

VLSID 2027: International Conference on VLSI Design

Sheraton Grand Whitefield, Bengaluru · in person and online
ConferenceVLSITape-out contest
What it is
India's top yearly conference on chip design (the 40th edition), held together with the 26th Conference on Embedded Systems. It is run by the VLSI Society of India.
What happens
Talks from industry and universities, research papers, an exhibition, and the national Tape-Out Contest for student chips.
Tape-Out Contest
For students whose chip was taped out in 2025 or 2026 at an Indian college. Entries closed on 30 Sep 2026. Finalists are told on 10 Nov 2026, and final posters are due 10 Dec 2026. 5 digital and 5 analog finalists are invited to the conference.
Who should go
VLSI students, M.Tech students and freshers aiming for RTL, verification or physical design jobs.
Why go
Hear what top chip companies in India are working on, and meet people from design teams in one place.
How to register
Check the official VLSID website (vlsid.org) for passes and student fees.

SEMICON India: next edition planned for March 2028

The 2026 edition was held 17–19 Sep 2026 at Yashobhoomi, New Delhi
ExpoBiggest in IndiaDate tentative
What it is
India's biggest chip industry expo, run by SEMI with the India Semiconductor Mission. Chip makers, equipment makers, government and colleges all come.
What happened in 2026
The 5th edition was opened by the Prime Minister in New Delhi. Many deals were signed there, including Tata Electronics' supplier partnerships for its Dholera fab.
What's next
SEMI's head said the next SEMICON India is tentatively fixed for March 2028. Dates may change.
Why it matters
It's the best place to see the whole Indian chip industry at once, from design to factories.

Online · free video lectures
BeginnerFreeOnline
What it is
A full online course from NPTEL (IITs and IISc) that teaches Verilog from the basics.
Who it's for
Students and freshers who are new to Verilog or want to revise before interviews.
How it works
Watch recorded lectures any time for free. NPTEL also runs it as a timed course with an exam and certificate in some semesters; check the NPTEL website for the next run.
Why do it
Verilog is the first skill asked in RTL and verification interviews.
