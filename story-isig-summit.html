Global chip leaders meet in New Delhi on 3–4 November

Chip company leaders, researchers and government officials will talk about packaging, testing and power chips.

Semicon Weekly · 1 November 2026
In short
  • The ISIG Executive Summit India 2026 will be held in New Delhi on 3 and 4 November.
  • Leaders from chip companies, researchers and government officials will attend.
  • Main topics: chip packaging, chip testing, and power chips made with SiC and GaN.

Key numbers

3–4 NovSummit dates
New DelhiVenue city
3Main topics: packaging, testing, power chips

What happened

  • The ISIG Executive Summit India 2026 was announced for 3–4 November in New Delhi.
  • It brings together chip industry leaders, researchers and policymakers to talk about India's chip ecosystem and supply chains.
  • Talks will cover how chips are packaged and tested, and new power chips made with silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN).
  • A senior official from MeitY (the IT ministry) is expected to attend.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Meetings like this help companies, colleges and the government plan together.
  • Packaging and testing are the first steps where India's new chip plants are starting work.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Packaging and power chips are the areas where India's new plants will hire first, so these are good topics to learn now.
  • Learn the basics: what a chip package is, how chips are tested (ATE), and why SiC/GaN chips handle high power.
  • Follow the summit news on LinkedIn to see which companies are active in India.

Why it is good for India

  • Strong packaging and testing skills let India do more of the chip work at home.
  • SiC and GaN chips are needed for EVs, solar power and fast chargers, all growing in India.

Words in this story

  • Packaging: putting a bare chip into a protective case and connecting it to the outside world.
  • SiC / GaN: silicon carbide and gallium nitride, materials for chips that handle high power and heat.
  • MeitY: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
