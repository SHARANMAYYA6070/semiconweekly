Bengaluru chip firm Tessolve raises $75 million

The Hero Electronix-owned company will use the money to buy other companies and grow.

Semicon Weekly · 3 November 2026
In short
  • Tessolve, a Bengaluru chip engineering company owned by Hero Electronix, has raised $75 million.
  • The round was led by US investor Vector Line Capital.
  • Tessolve plans to use the money to buy other companies over the next few months.

Key numbers

$75 millionMoney raised
BengaluruWhere Tessolve is based
3Main services: design, testing, validation

What happened

  • Tessolve raised $75 million in new funding, led by US investor Vector Line Capital.
  • Tessolve is owned by Hero Electronix.
  • The company plans to use the money to buy other companies in the coming months.
  • Tessolve does chip design, testing and validation work for AI, computer and car chips.

Why it matters

Why this is useful

  • Fresh money lets a company grow faster and add new skills by buying other firms.
  • Chip service companies help many global chip makers finish their products.

Why it is good for freshers

  • Indian chip service companies are growing fast, and they hire many freshers for design, verification and testing work.
  • Tessolve-type companies are a common first job for VLSI freshers.
  • Skills to build: Verilog, verification basics, and chip testing (ATE, DFT).

Why it is good for India

  • A Bengaluru company growing and buying others shows India's chip services are getting stronger.
  • More services work stays in India, building deep chip skills here.

Words in this story

  • Validation: checking a real chip works correctly in real conditions.
  • Acquisition: when one company buys another.
