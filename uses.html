Each use, explained

PIN 01

Mobile phones

What it does
Calls, camera, apps and UPI all run on chips.
Chips inside
A processor (the brain), memory to store photos and apps, a modem for calls and internet, camera sensors, and power chips for the battery.
India link
India is one of the world's biggest makers of mobile phones, and many phones sold abroad are now assembled here.
Jobs linked
SoC design, verification, embedded software, RF engineering · See jobs →
PIN 02

Computers and laptops

What it does
The processor and memory inside are chips.
Chips inside
A CPU, sometimes a graphics chip (GPU), RAM and SSD memory, and chips for Wi-Fi, USB and power.
India link
The government is pushing companies to make more laptops and servers in India.
Jobs linked
CPU and GPU design, verification, physical design · See jobs →
PIN 03

Cars and electric vehicles

What it does
Chips control the engine, battery, brakes and airbags.
Chips inside
Microcontrollers for each system, power chips for the battery and motor, sensors for cameras and radar, and chips for the dashboard screen.
India link
India is a huge car and two-wheeler market, and electric vehicles need even more chips than petrol ones.
Jobs linked
Embedded, automotive software, power electronics, functional safety · See jobs →
PIN 04

Hospitals and medical devices

What it does
ECG machines, scanners and pulse meters use chips.
Chips inside
Sensor chips that read the body's signals, converters that turn them into numbers, and processors that show the results.
India link
Indian startups are building low-cost medical devices that need these chips.
Jobs linked
Analog design, embedded systems, signal processing · See jobs →
PIN 05

Solar power

What it does
Solar panels and inverters are built on semiconductors.
Chips inside
A solar cell is itself a semiconductor that turns light into electricity. Inverters use power chips to send it to your home.
India link
India is adding a lot of solar power and making more solar panels at home.
Jobs linked
Power electronics, process engineering, testing · See jobs →
PIN 06

LED lights

What it does
An LED is a small semiconductor that gives light.
Chips inside
The LED chip glows when current passes, and a small driver chip controls the current.
India link
Government programmes have put LED bulbs into crores of Indian homes to save power.
Jobs linked
Device engineering, power and driver design · See jobs →
PIN 07

TV and home appliances

What it does
Washing machines, ACs and fridges have chips inside.
Chips inside
Microcontrollers run the buttons and timers, motor-control chips save power, and Wi-Fi chips make them smart.
India link
Many TVs, ACs and washing machines sold in India are made here.
Jobs linked
Embedded C, motor control, IoT · See jobs →
PIN 08

Internet and 5G

What it does
Towers and routers use chips to send data.
Chips inside
Radio-frequency (RF) chips send and receive signals, and network processors move the data fast.
India link
India rolled out 5G across the country, which needs huge numbers of these chips in towers and phones.
Jobs linked
RF design, communication systems, DSP · See jobs →
PIN 09

AI and data centres

What it does
Powerful chips train and run AI.
Chips inside
GPUs and special AI accelerator chips do the maths, with very fast memory beside them.
India link
India is building more data centres and AI computing power for its own AI models.
Jobs linked
Accelerator design, verification, high-speed interfaces · See jobs →
PIN 10

Satellites and space

What it does
Chips control satellites and rockets.
Chips inside
Space chips must survive radiation and extreme heat and cold, so they are specially designed and tested.
India link
ISRO's satellites and rockets need these tough chips, and India's space startups are growing.
Jobs linked
Rad-hard design, testing, embedded systems · See jobs →
PIN 11

Defence

What it does
Radar, communication and navigation need chips.
Chips inside
High-power RF chips for radar, secure processors for communication, and chips for missiles and drones.
India link
India wants defence electronics to be made at home for safety reasons.
Jobs linked
RF, FPGA design, embedded, security · See jobs →
PIN 12

Trains and railways

What it does
Signals and train control use chips.
Chips inside
Electronic signalling, train-protection systems, and motor drives in electric trains all use chips.
India link
Indian Railways is adding automatic train-protection and electric trains, which need electronics.
Jobs linked
Embedded, safety systems, power electronics · See jobs →
PIN 13

Banking and payments

What it does
ATMs, card machines and debit cards have chips.
Chips inside
Your debit card has a small secure chip. Card machines and ATMs have processors and security chips.
India link
UPI and card payments in India run on phones and machines full of chips.
Jobs linked
Secure chip design, embedded, verification · See jobs →
PIN 14

Farming

What it does
Soil sensors and smart pumps use chips.
Chips inside
Sensor chips measure soil moisture and weather, and small controllers switch pumps on and off.
India link
Farm drones and smart irrigation are growing in India to save water and labour.
Jobs linked
IoT, embedded systems, sensor design · See jobs →
PIN 15

Smart watches and earbuds

What it does
Tiny chips track health and play music.
Chips inside
A low-power processor, Bluetooth chip, heart-rate and motion sensors, and a battery-management chip.
India link
India is one of the biggest markets for smart watches and earbuds.
Jobs linked
Low-power design, Bluetooth, embedded · See jobs →
